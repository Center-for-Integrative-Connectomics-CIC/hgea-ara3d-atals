Accuracy
Placement: distance from each plate's printed ARA lines to the 3D ARA annotation on its slice. Fill: each inner plate hidden and predicted from its neighbours (a 2× gap, so conservative).
Method
Each of the 33 HGEA coronal plates is placed on its own slice of the 3D ARA (the brain the plates were drawn on); slices follow one line from the Allen section numbers. Between plates, both neighbours' domains are carried to each slice along the Nissl and blended by distance. No CCFv3 or BBP mapping yet.
